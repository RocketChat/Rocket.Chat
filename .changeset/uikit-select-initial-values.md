Adds initial selections to the UiKit users, channels and conversations selects: `initial_user`/`initial_users` (usernames), `initial_channel`/`initial_channels` and `initial_conversation`/`initial_conversations` (room ids). Selected entries now keep their chips even when the current search does not return them.
