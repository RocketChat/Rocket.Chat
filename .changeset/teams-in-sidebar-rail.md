Adds a Teams section to the sidebar when the sidebar rail feature preview is on: it lists the user's teams with the rooms they joined in each, and a search filters teams and rooms by name. Each team has a menu to open its info, members and channels, leave it, move it up or down, and keep its unread rooms in view or on top. The rail gets a Teams item and an Inbox item in place of Home, both with unread badges, and Sidebar preferences in place of Display
