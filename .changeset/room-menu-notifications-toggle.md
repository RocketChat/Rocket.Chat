Adds a "Notifications" toggle to the room menu in the sidebar, the switch the sidepanel menu already had, and marks a room whose notifications are off with a crossed bell after its title in the sidebar and the sidepanel. The hide room confirmation is reworded: "Hide room" as title and button, and a body that says the room comes back when opened or on a new message.
