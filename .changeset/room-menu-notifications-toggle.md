Adds a "Notifications" toggle to the room menu in the sidebar, the switch the sidepanel menu already had, and marks a room whose notifications are off with a crossed bell after its title in the sidebar and the sidepanel. The hide room confirmation is reworded: "Hide room" as title and button (the same key names the `/hide` slash command), a body that says the room leaves the sidebar and comes back when opened or on a new message, and "Don't ask again" on the checkbox every do-not-ask-again modal shares.
