Adds the UiKit `task_card` and `plan` blocks and the `url` source element, for apps that show the progress of a multi-step task, such as an AI agent. A task card has a title, a status (`pending`, `in_progress`, `complete` or `error`), optional details and output, and links to its sources; a plan shows up to 50 tasks together under a title. An app updates the message or view to change their statuses.
