`IconButton` takes `variant` and `size`, like `Button`. `variant` is one of `primary`, `secondary`, `info`, `danger`, `warning`, `success`, `secondary-danger`, `secondary-warning` or `secondary-success`; `size` is one of `mini`, `tiny`, `small`, `medium` or `large`. The boolean props they replace (`primary`, `secondary`, `info`, `danger`, `warning`, `success`, `mini`, `tiny`, `small`, `medium`, `large`) are deprecated and keep working. `variant='primary'` looks the way `primary` and `secondary` with `info` did.
