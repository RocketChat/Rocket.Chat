Renders the L3 forms, list and select components with Tamagui frames compiled to static CSS: the Item family (`Item` and its parts), `Option`, `Options`, `OptionsPaginated`, `Listbox` and `Menu`; `Label`, `Field`, `FieldGroup` and their parts; `InputBox`, the inputs built on it and its wrapper, addon and skeleton; and `Select`, `SelectLegacy`, `SelectFiltered`, `MultiSelect`, `MultiSelectFiltered`, `AutoComplete` and the paginated selects. Their look is unchanged, with the exceptions listed below.

A closed options popup now unmounts after its hide animation: `Options` and `OptionsPaginated` forward `onAnimationEnd`, which `AnimatedVisibility` needs.

### Breaking for npm consumers

- `style` is no longer accepted by any of these components and no longer reaches their elements (`Select`, `Item` and its parts, `FieldHint`, `FieldGroup`, `TextAreaInput`, the options lists). The exceptions are the style `Position` hands a part it places (`Options`, `OptionsPaginated`, the select popup) and the offsets a virtualizer hands a list and its rows. `OptionContainer` takes `width` instead of `style`.
- Props narrow to the attributes of the element each part renders plus the variations it declares, so an attribute outside that list no longer type-checks, and one passed anyway is not forwarded. `Select` no longer takes the input-only props (`readOnly`, `autoComplete`, `type`) it never forwarded, and the select parts take the Fuselage attribute groups instead of every HTML attribute.
- `is` is a closed set: `Item` (`div`, `li`, `a`, `label`), `ItemGroup` (`div`, `ul`, `ol`), `ItemGroupHeader`, `ItemDivider` and `ItemDropSlot` (`div`, `li`), `ItemGroupTitle` (`div`, `button`, `h2`, `h3`, `h4`), `ItemTitle` (`div`, `span`, `label`), `ItemDescription` and `ItemMeta` (`div`, `span`), `ItemLink` (`a`), `Option` (`li`, `div`) and `Label` (`label`, `span`). `Item`'s `is` was any element type before. `InputBox`'s `type` narrows to the types in use (text, email, url, search, password, number, date, time, datetime-local, color, file, textarea): `button`, `checkbox`, `datetime`, `hidden`, `image`, `month`, `radio`, `range`, `reset`, `submit`, `tel`, `week` and `select` no longer type-check, `InputBox` loses `input` and `multiple`, and `Input`'s `is` loses `select`.
- `Message`, `MessageSystem`, `SidebarItem` and its parts, `SidebarGroupTitle` and the `Option*` parts take the `Item` attribute groups instead of every HTML attribute of a `div`.
- `Item`: `variant` also takes `success`, `warning` and `primary`, and `ItemActions` takes `justify`; rows that hold an `ItemLink`, and the sidebar group header, pass `interactive`; the sidebar rows keep their height through `ItemRow fixedHeight`; `wrap` lets the slots wrap onto a second line. `ItemProps` is joined by `ItemAttributes`, and `ItemPartAttributes`, `ItemActionsContext`, `ItemRowContext` and `useReportOpen` are new exports of the Item modules.
- `Label` also exports `LabelBase` and `LabelContext`; `Options` no longer exports `optionsCustomProperties`, and `OptionsSize` and `toCSSSize` move to `optionsSize`.
- `Menu` and `Options`: the maximum width and height are custom properties (`--rcx-menu-max-width`, `--rcx-options-max-height`, `--rcx-options-width`) their frames read.
- `Field` and `FieldGroup`: `FieldGroup` spaces its children with `gap` and no longer adds `rcx-field-group__item` to them, so a stack of fields ends 48px shorter than before in the account profile; the Field description, hint, error and link carry `rcx-box--with-inline-elements` instead of their own rules.
- `InputBox`: a `textarea` no longer carries the `size` attribute. The border, background, text, caret and addon colours of every state come from one generated stylesheet keyed on the part's classes and on native state, so an app's own input-like box keeps `rcx-input-box__wrapper` for its colours and sets its own layout. `Input` takes `undecorated`, `wrapped`, `last` and `placeholderVisible`; the classes `rcx-input-box--undecorated` and `rcx-input-box--small` no longer style anything on a part that does not render an `Input`.
- `Select` family: the container is a `SelectBox` with the same classes as before (`rcx-select`, `rcx-autocomplete` and their modifiers), the filter input is `SelectFilter` and keeps `rcx-select__focus` and `rcx-select__filter`, and the multiple selects' anchor keeps `rcx-select__anchor--last`. The wrapper, field and values no longer carry negative margins; the box carries the padding instead. A `div` select no longer carries the `disabled` attribute.
