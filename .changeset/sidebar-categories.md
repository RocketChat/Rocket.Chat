Added user-defined sidebar categories: collapsible folders that group channels in the sidebar. Create a category from the **+** (Create new) menu, optionally selecting channels to include, add more channels from the category menu, and delete a category to move its channels back to the main list. Categories are private to each user and persisted locally in the browser (a future iteration will sync them across devices and add drag-and-drop).
