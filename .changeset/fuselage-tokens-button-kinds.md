Adds the button colors of the `warning`, `secondary-warning` and `secondary-success` kinds (`backgroundWarningDefault`, `fontOnSecondarySuccess`…) and the `extra-light-error` shadow. In light they are the colors Fuselage already gave them. In dark, the `secondary-warning` and `secondary-success` backgrounds are those of `secondary`, their text is `y200` and `g200` (`y1000` and `g1000` when disabled), a disabled `warning` is `y1000` with `#757575` text, and the `extra-light-error` shadow is the `extraLightError` stroke, `#f49aa6`. In high contrast, the `secondary-warning` and `secondary-success` text is `y1000` and `g1000`
