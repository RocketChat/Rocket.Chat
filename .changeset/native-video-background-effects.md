Adds background effects to native video calls: blur at three strengths, preset pictures and pictures of your own, chosen in a backgrounds panel before joining or during the call, with a quick toggle in the camera menu, and remembered for your next call. Screen readers now hear which device and which choice is in use in the device menus.
