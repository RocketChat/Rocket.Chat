Adds an activity filter to sidebar groups and custom categories: an expanded group can hide read conversations with no activity in the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, while unread ones always stay listed, a chip next to the group name shows the window and, when clicked, lifts the filter for the session or puts it back
