Adds `Text`, which sets text in one of the design system's font scales (`fontScale`) and text or status colours (`color`), and can keep it on one line cut with an ellipsis (`truncate`). It renders a `div` unless `is` names the element the text is (`p`, `h2`, `label`, `span`…), and takes no `Box` styling props.
