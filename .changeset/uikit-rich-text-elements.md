Adds the `color`, `tag`, `citation`, `message_mention`, `attachment_mention`, `usergroup` and `team` inline elements to the UiKit `rich_text` block. Tags are drawn as chips, citations as numbered links, and message, team and user group mentions resolve their targets by id.
