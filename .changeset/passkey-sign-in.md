Adds sign-in with passkeys, behind the new "Enable passkeys" setting (Accounts → Passkeys), off by default. Users add passkeys under Account → Passkeys after confirming their second factor or password, and sign in with them from the login page without a password or 2FA code. Passkeys are bound to the domain of the Site URL.
