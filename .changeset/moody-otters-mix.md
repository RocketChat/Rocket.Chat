Adds a bubble to the sidebar room list, shown when an unread conversation is scrolled out of view. It reads "Unread mentions" when a conversation in that direction mentions you, and "More unreads" otherwise. Clicking it scrolls to the nearest mention in that direction, or to the nearest unread room if there is no mention.
