Adds a reusable hover card: `useHoverCard` and `HoverCardPopover` for the hover intent (open and close delays, hand-off between triggers, Escape, staying open while the card's own menu is open, no hover on devices that can't hover), and `HoverCard`, `HoverCardBand`, `HoverCardSection`, `HoverCardHeader`, `HoverCardInfoList`, `HoverCardInfoItem` and `HoverCardActions` for its layout. The user card is built on it.
