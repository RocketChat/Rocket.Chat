Adds the extra-light error shadow to the palette `PaletteStyleTag` writes: `--rcx-color-shadow-extra-light-error`

In dark, the `secondary-warning` and `secondary-success` buttons, the disabled `warning` button and the focus ring of the danger buttons take colors of their own instead of the light ones; in high contrast, the text of the `secondary-warning` and `secondary-success` buttons is darker
