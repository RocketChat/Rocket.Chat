# Navigation

Components that move the user around the app, or offer them a choice of actions or options.

## Lists and menus

- The actions the user can take at a given moment, behind a trigger: [`Menu`](?path=/docs/navigation-menu--docs)
- A row of a list, composed from media, an icon, content and actions: [`Item`](?path=/docs/navigation-item--docs).
  Lists whose rows can be reordered are covered in [Drag and drop](?path=/docs/navigation-item-drag-and-drop--docs).
- A panel of choices floating next to the element that opened it: [`Dropdown`](?path=/docs/navigation-dropdown--docs)
- The options inside a select or a dropdown: [`Option`](?path=/docs/navigation-option--docs), in a list of
  [`Options`](?path=/docs/navigation-options--docs), or [`OptionsPaginated`](?path=/docs/navigation-options-optionspaginated--docs)
  when they load page by page

## Moving between content

- Groups of related content on the same screen: [`Tabs`](?path=/docs/navigation-tabs--docs)
- Data split into pages, usually under a table: [`Pagination`](?path=/docs/navigation-pagination--docs)

## The app's frame

- The global header: [`NavBar`](?path=/docs/navigation-navbar--docs)
- The room list, grouped into collapsible categories: [`Sidebar`](?path=/docs/navigation-sidebar--docs), with its
  [items](?path=/docs/navigation-sidebar-item--docs) and [media](?path=/docs/navigation-sidebar-media--docs)
- A secondary panel next to the sidebar: [`Sidepanel`](?path=/docs/navigation-sidepanel--docs)
