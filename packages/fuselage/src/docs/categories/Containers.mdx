# Containers

Surfaces that group content and give it a place on screen.

- A piece of content presented on its own surface, with a title, a body and actions:
  [`Card`](?path=/docs/containers-card--docs). Arrange several with [`CardGroup`](?path=/docs/containers-cardgroup--docs),
  or in responsive columns with [`CardGrid`](?path=/docs/containers-cardgrid--docs).
- A raised surface with no structure of its own: [`Tile`](?path=/docs/containers-tile--docs).
- Sections the user expands and collapses under their titles: [`Accordion`](?path=/docs/containers-accordion--docs).
- Information and actions about the current page, beside it, without leaving it:
  [`Contextualbar`](?path=/docs/containers-contextualbar--docs).
- A prompt that blocks the rest of the screen until the user answers it: [`Modal`](?path=/docs/containers-modal--docs).
