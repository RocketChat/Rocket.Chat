Project requirements:
    Prerequuisites:
        -Git
        -Python3 
            (python 3.13.11, system version, no venv/conda)

    Dependencies:
        - NodeJS:v22.16.0 
            (pacman) 
        - Yarn:v4.12.0 
            (pacman) 
        - Meteor:v3.4  
            (curl https://install.meteor.com/\?release\=3.4 | sh)
        - Deno:v2.3.1 
            (curl -fsSL https://deno.land/install.sh | sh -s v2.3.1)

    Reference Documentation: https://developer.rocket.chat/docs/linux-and-windows-rocketchat-development-environment

Build / Run (local):
    to build: 
        `cd RocketChat`
        `yarn`
    to run:
        'yarn dsv'
